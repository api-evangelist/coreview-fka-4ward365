During onboarding, CoreView connects to the Microsoft tenant by using service accounts. These accounts are required to establish connectivity and allow imports to run during onboarding and after onboarding. The “ Admin read-only ” page displays the service accounts used for this connection and allows Tenant Admins to manage them after onboarding.
