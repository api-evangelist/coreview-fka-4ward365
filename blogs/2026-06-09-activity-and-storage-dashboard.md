The “ Activity & storage ” dashboard provides an overview of SharePoint storage usage across the tenant. It highlights current storage consumption, storage trends, projected growth, reclaimable storage, and the sites or items contributing most to storage usage. The dashboard also shows storage that can potentially be reclaimed through supported policy remediation workflows.
