To begin, click on “ REPORTS ” in the sidebar to access a comprehensive menu. This includes sections for “ Custom ”, “ Scheduled ”, and “ Exported ” reports, along with a variety of predefined reports . Clicking on each item will open an additional menu, revealing more report options: Each report or section provides access to different features, enabling you to organize, manage, and view your reports.
