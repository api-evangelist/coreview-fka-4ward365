The “ Granted Access ” panel lets Tenant Admins and delegated operators review and manage permissions for SharePoint files and folders, including both email and link-based access. Access and navigation Open the panel by clicking the name of any listed item. Navigate between items using up/down arrows ; the panel remains open.
