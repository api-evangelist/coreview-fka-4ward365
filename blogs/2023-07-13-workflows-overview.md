CoreView Workflows automates administrative tasks in Microsoft 365 environments by configuring chains of actions that perform repetitive management functions without manual intervention. A Workflow consists of a sequence of actions configured to execute specific tasks. A common implementation is the user provisioning and deprovisioning process.
