CoreView's management actions refer to the following categories: Users Add tag(s) for categorization selected user(s) Add tags to categorize users within CoreView, i.e., to identify users who are participating in a pilot program. Tags are unique to CoreView and will not be written back to Azure Active Directory. Can be performed in bulk to multiple users.
