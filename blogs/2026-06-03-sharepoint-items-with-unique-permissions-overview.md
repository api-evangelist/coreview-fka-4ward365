Managing permissions for SharePoint files and folders allows you to review access assignments on documents and folders. When users share content by granting direct access or creating sharing links, permission inheritance can be broken, creating unique permissions on individual documents or folders. Unique permissions can create access assignments that differ from the parent site, library, or folder and therefore require separate review.
