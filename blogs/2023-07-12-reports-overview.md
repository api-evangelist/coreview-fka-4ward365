CoreView reports give you access to data from your Microsoft 365 environment, including users, licenses, security status, activity across different services and more. Reports are organized by category and can be filtered, exported, customized, or scheduled for delivery. Types of reports Reports can be divided into: Predefined reports Custom reports , customized reports you saved or publicly shared customized reports.
