As a delegated operator, you can use the Privileges panel to select your operational scope across all assigned resources, including Virtual Tenants , Number Pools , and License Pools . This is useful if you work across different organizational units—such as agencies, hospitals, or departments—where you may have varying levels of access. For example, you may have full management rights in one Virtual Tenant , while only having read-only access in another.
