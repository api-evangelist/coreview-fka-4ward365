The “ Support access ” feature allows Tenant Administrators to temporarily grant CoreView operators , including Account Managers or Support Team members, access to the tenant environment . Access configurations include specific roles, permissions, virtual tenants (if available), and expiration dates. Delegated operators can only assign access within their own permission scope.
