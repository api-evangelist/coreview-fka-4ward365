This guide will assist you in finding and selecting the correct time zone under “ My profile ”: Simply press Ctrl/Cmd+F and enter your time zone, which will correspond to a city. Once you have identified a city that shares the same time zone as yours from the list, return to “ Personal info > Time zone ” in the “ My profile ” section. Select the city you found on this list from the dropdown.
