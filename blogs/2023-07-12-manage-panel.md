The “ Manage ” panel is a tool that provides you with quick access to a range of actions and settings, and dynamically adapts based on various factors, including the user's role, the type of report being viewed, and the items selected. You can access this tool by clicking on the banner located on the right side of the screen. Hidden manage panel.
