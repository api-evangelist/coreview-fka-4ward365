Understanding policy thresholds Before exploring the Governance Center, it is essential to understand two key concepts: what a policy threshold is and how you can configure it. What is a policy threshold? A policy threshold is defined as the maximum number of matched items (policy violations) that a policy can have before it is considered non-compliant.
