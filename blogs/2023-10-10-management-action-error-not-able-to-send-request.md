Problem When you try to perform a management action, you encounter the following error message in the management action window: “ Sorry, the operation required cannot be satisfied, if you are using an existing session try to close your browser and connect again ”. Explanation This error is related to the session you are using to connect to CoreView, and most often happens when your token expires while performing a management action . The CoreView authentication token has a 3-day expiry policy.
