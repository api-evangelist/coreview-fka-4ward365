Certificate-Based Authentication (CBA) is required to keep Exchange Online management actions running through the Exchange Online PowerShell module. Microsoft is retiring the Credential parameter in Exchange Online PowerShell in favor of modern authentication. For more information, see Microsoft’s announcement about the Exchange Online PowerShell deprecation .
