Here is the list of reports available in CoreView Control for SharePoint and their scope. Report Description SharePoint site drilldowns For each SharePoint site in the tenant, it shows per-site aggregates for unique permissions, external exposure, and versioning and storage hotspots. SharePoint item-level Provides detailed information for any SharePoint Online files and folders, including items with or without unique permissions.
