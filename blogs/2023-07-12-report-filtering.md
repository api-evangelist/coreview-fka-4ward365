The “ Report filters ” functionality allows you to filter properties to obtain results tailored to your specific needs. Filtering reports allows for the use of AND and OR conditions, providing a more flexible way to manipulate data and enabling users to apply multiple conditions simultaneously. This functionality is particularly useful when you need to filter data based on more than one criterion, which is achievable through the use of AND and OR operators.
