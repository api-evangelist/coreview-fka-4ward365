As a member of CoreCommunity, you can download, share, and submit workflows and custom actions you have built into the libraries. Downloading a workflow or a custom action Simply visit the specific section, click on the workflow/custom action title, and download the zip file. Inside the zip file, you will find the JSON files that you can upload to your CoreView app.
