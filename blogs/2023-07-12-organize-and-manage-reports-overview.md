Regardless of the type of report you are working with, there are several features you can use to manage and organize your report. They are: Subscribe/unsubscribe : this feature allows you to subscribe to scheduled reports. Duplicate : this feature allows you to make copies of your scheduled reports, providing a starting point for new analyses without altering the original data.
