When the “ Hide sensitive data ” option is enabled in Settings under “ Organization settings ”, the User Principal Name (UPN) in User reports is hidden, thereby concealing the identity of the individual . Furthermore, the following statistical data will not be available for users: SharePointActivityActiveFiles30 SharePointActivityFilesSynced30 SharePointActivityFilesSharedInternally30 SharePointActivityFilesSharedExternally30 OneDriveActivityActiveFiles30 OneDriveActivityFilesSynced30 OneDriveActivityFilesSharedInternally30 OneDriveActivityFilesSharedExternally30 OneDriveSiteUsageFilesViewedOr
