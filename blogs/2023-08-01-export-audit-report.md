The “ Export ” functionality allows for easy sharing of reports outside of the platform and facilitates further analysis in other tools. To utilize the " Export " feature, follow these steps: Select the Audit report you wish to export. Access the export function: navigate to the top right corner of the interface and click on the “ Export” button.
