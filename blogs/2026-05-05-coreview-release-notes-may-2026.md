May 26 enhancements [SETTINGS] Service account management modes in Admin Read Only : in “Organization Settings > Admin read only ”, service account management is now split into “Manual” and “Automatic” modes through a dedicated toggle. “Manual” mode lets Tenant Admins update service account usernames and passwords. “Automatic” mode lets Tenant Admins create or recreate service accounts.
