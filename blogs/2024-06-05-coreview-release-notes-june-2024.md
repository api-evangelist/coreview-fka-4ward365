June 19 enhancements [DEVICE REPORTS] “Execute workflow” action in Device reports We've introduced a direct “ Execute workflow ” action in the Manage panel of each Device report, allowing you to initiate workflows without leaving the report page. Enjoy simplified and faster device management! [PLAYBOOKS] Seamless policy reassignment on operator deletion A new pop-up window now alerts you when deleting operators who own policies, offering a dropdown for easy reassignment to other admins.
