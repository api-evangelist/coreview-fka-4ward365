As a tenant admin, you can assign precise, workload-focused permissions to operators. This allows each operator to manage only the workloads relevant to their responsibilities in the tenant you are setting their permissions for. For example, if you set permissions in the “Dev Tenant,” those permissions will only apply to that specific tenant.
