For some report categories, CoreView provides graphical dashboards arranged on a single screen so the information can be monitored at a glance. Here, you are provided with an overview of key performance indicators or metrics relevant to your Office 365 environment. Dashboards : the reports section includes four graphical dashboards that provide at-a-glance views of key performance indicators or metrics.
