Publishing a Workflow for the first time allows delegated admins with the necessary permissions to view, edit, and potentially execute it. How to publish a Workflow Step 1 “ Save ” the Workflow. Step 2 The saved Workflow will appear as “ Draft ” in the “ Manage Workflow” section.
