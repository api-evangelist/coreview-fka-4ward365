Configuration Manager provides default compliance and change detection reports as a starting point for monitoring your cloud environment. For more specific requirements, you can customize the reporting and set up targeted alerts thanks to the integration of Power BI Reporting with Log Analytics. This feature enables you to adjust your monitoring approach based on your needs.
