This section encompasses all aspects of Workflow management, including its creation, execution, and monitoring. Basics of Workflow management The primary actions related to Workflow management are: Workflow list : see the Workflows delegated to you and review past executions. Workflow preview : verify the actions that will be executed before running a Workflow.
