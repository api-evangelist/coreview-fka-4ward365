The “ Sharing and access ” dashboard summarizes how SharePoint sites are configured for sharing and external access. It shows how SharePoint sites are distributed across different sharing combinations and identifies configurations that may require review. It also provides access to filtered reports for reviewing sites with higher-risk sharing configurations.
