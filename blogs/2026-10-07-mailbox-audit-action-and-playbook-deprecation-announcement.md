Important notice We want to inform you about a significant update regarding the Mailbox audit Management Action and Playbook. Since January 2019 , Microsoft has enabled mailbox auditing by default at the organization level . This setting overrides the per-mailbox setting used by the retired controls, so they no longer change mailbox auditing behavior.
