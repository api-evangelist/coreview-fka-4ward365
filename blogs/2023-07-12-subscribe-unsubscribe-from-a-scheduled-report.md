The “ Subscriptions ” feature allows you to subscribe or unsubscribe to scheduled reports , providing a convenient method to maintain an organized workflow. To subscribe or unsubscribe from a report, you need to interact with the checkbox located under the “ Subscribed ” column. The state of this checkbox indicates the subscription status: A white checkbox signifies that you are not currently subscribed to the report.
