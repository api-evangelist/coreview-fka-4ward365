CoreView Access Reviews enable Tenant Admins and delegated Access Review Admins to assess and adjust user permissions across Microsoft 365 resources on a scheduled basis. Admins can identify and remove obsolete user permissions, and configure Access Reviews to align with internal policies or regulatory requirements, such as NIST, ISO, or SOC2. Reviewer assignments, notifications, and reporting occur through automated CoreView workflows .
