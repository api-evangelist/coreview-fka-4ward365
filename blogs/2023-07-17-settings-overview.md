The “ Settings ” section is a centralized hub that primarily allows Tenant Admins to access and manage all sections. For this, reports under Settings cannot be filtered by Virtual Tenants. Operators have visibility of the “ Settings ” menu, but their access is limited to the “My Organization” section only, and they can view the organization information in read-only mode.
