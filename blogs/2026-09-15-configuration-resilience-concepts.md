Before exploring Configuration Resilience, it is essential to understand the following key concepts: Target tenant The target tenant is the tenant where a deployment is applied . It is always the tenant you are signed in to , which is the tenant selected under “Managed tenants” in the top bar. To deploy configurations to a different tenant, select that tenant first.
